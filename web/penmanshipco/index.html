ThePenmanshipCo

Logo and social media branding for a copywriting agency.

Overview

ThePenmanshipCo is a professional copywriting agency that provides expert writing services to businesses and individuals. They were looking for a fresh brand identity that communicates their expertise and professionalism.

Solution

I created a minimal yet modern logo and a few social media templates that capture the essence of the brand. The new design gives the agency a sleek, professional look that has been well received by its audience.

Logo

The logo is built from the starting letters of the brand name (T, P and C), with an additional nib added for aesthetics.

Typography

Stardos Stencil is used for the wordmark, Lora for headings and Roboto for general text, all from Google Fonts.

Colour and social media

The palette pairs two blues with a soft rose and an off-white, and it carries through to the social media templates.

  • Dark Tyrian Blue #0D2B52
  • YinMn Blue #405774
  • Misty Rose #F5E2E0
  • Moon White #EAF4FC
