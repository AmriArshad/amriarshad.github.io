KiwiVanman

Service: Website design
Industry: Camping and vehicles
Year: 2023

Overview

Kiwi Vanman, also known as KVM Camper Vans, is a New Zealand business that specialises in crafting luxury mini camper vans, combining travel and accommodation in one vehicle.

The problem

KVM had carved out a niche with its high-quality vans, but their website wasn't doing them justice. It wasn't set up to show off the vans or their range of camping gear, and it didn't represent the brand well. They needed a redesign that would engage customers and make buying online possible.

The solution

I carried out a comprehensive redesign, fixing design flaws and improving the overall experience. The new design highlights the beauty and features of the vans and includes an e-commerce shop, so customers can buy camping gear easily.
